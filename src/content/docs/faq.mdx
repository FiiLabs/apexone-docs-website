## How is this different from a regular VPS?

Claude Code comes installed and you can buy a Claude account with the machine; the disk is encrypted and unreadable even to us while suspended; and you can turn on auto-suspend so idle time costs only the base fee. Always on, it costs about the same as a comparable cloud server.

## Will suspending lose what I'm running?

No. Suspending saves the whole memory to disk and loads it back exactly; processes and open files are all there. New machines don't auto-suspend by default, and when it's on an active machine isn't suspended. Note that cron and other scheduled jobs don't run while suspended.

## How do I connect to a suspended machine?

Just SSH in. When the connection arrives we start the machine and forward it — about 10 extra seconds.

## Can I run a public service?

Yes, but on `server-IP:port` rather than a dedicated IP — open a port on the machine page. Custom domains and automatic HTTPS aren't supported yet.

## Do the pre-installed AI tools come with an account?

Not by default — sign in yourself; we never touch your credentials. Or buy Claude Pro at creation (+$40 per 30 days) and we sign it in for you.

## Will you charge me automatically?

We never charge your payment method automatically. The only recurring deduction is a Claude account you bought: every 30 days from your balance.

## What happens when my balance runs out?

Machines are suspended and data is kept — top up and carry on. After 7 days in debt machines can't be started; only after 30 days and repeated notices are they deleted.
