## Common errors

### 401 Unauthorized

- The API key is invalid, expired, or revoked.
- The base URL is incorrect — it must be `https://api.apex1.us` (or `https://api.apex1.us/v1` for OpenAI-SDK-style clients).
- The auth file (`auth.json` / `settings.json`) is malformed JSON.

### Unable to connect

- Confirm your network can reach `https://api.apex1.us`.
- Check for corporate proxies or firewalls blocking outbound HTTPS.

### Model not found

- Verify the model name against the [Supported Models](/#supported-models) list on the Overview.
- Confirm the model is included in your current plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is my data used for training?

No. Customer prompts and responses are **never** used for model training. Want to verify for yourself that no middle party — including us — can even read them? See [Privacy Proof](/verification/privacy-proof/).

### Do I need separate keys for different tools?

No. A single ApexOne API key works across Claude Code, OpenCode, Cursor, Cline, and direct API calls. You can create multiple keys in the Dashboard to separate usage by project.

### How do I monitor usage and costs?

The [ApexOne Dashboard](https://apex1.us) shows all requests, token usage, costs, and cache statistics in real time — with a verifiable inference receipt for every call.

### What happens when an upstream provider goes down?

ApexOne's automatic failover reroutes traffic in the background, so your application keeps receiving responses with no code changes. Every failover is recorded in your receipts.

### How do I get enterprise access?

Contact **support@apex1.us** for enterprise plans, custom SLAs, and volume pricing.

## Sharing your subscription

### Can the person whose subscription served my request read my prompt?

No — not on the shared-subscription route. Providers contribute **quota**, not servers: the request
leaves our TEE and goes straight to the upstream vendor, so it never touches a provider's device.
They only see that their quota was consumed.

There is one route where prompts do reach third-party infrastructure — the provider-supplied API
relay — and it is listed explicitly in [how your requests are served](/#how-your-requests-are-served).
Site operators can disable that route entirely.

### How much can I earn by sharing my subscription?

We commit to the **revenue share** — 80% of what the platform charges the user — and to paying it
out on-chain with no withdrawal fee. We don't commit to an amount: that depends on real demand, and
the platform is early with demand still building. See
[Share Your Subscription](/earn/share-subscription/) for the formula.

### Why can't I withdraw my earnings yet?

Earnings sit in a **72-hour hold** before they become withdrawable — it covers the window in which a
consumer's card payment can still be disputed. See
[Earnings & Withdrawals](/earn/earnings-and-withdrawals/).

### My account is connected but isn't earning anything.

Check its state first. An account in the **review period** is not serving traffic yet — it joins the
pool only after passing consecutive probes and clearing the minimum observation window. An account
that *is* serving but earning nothing simply has no demand routed to it.
