Machine page → **External access**: enter the port inside the machine and the protocol (TCP / UDP); an external port between **20000 and 29999** is assigned. Traffic to `server-IP:external-port` reaches that port on your machine.

- We only forward bytes; we can't see the content of TCP sessions.
- Custom domains and automatic HTTPS aren't supported yet.
- Your machines cannot reach each other (on purpose: one compromised machine can't reach the next).
- Outbound port 25 is blocked, so you can't send mail directly; 465 / 587 work.

<Aside type="caution" title="UDP">
	UDP can't trigger a wake. Machines with a UDP mapping never auto-suspend — otherwise your UDP service would silently go dark once the machine slept.
</Aside>
