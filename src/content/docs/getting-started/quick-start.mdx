<Steps>

1. **Sign up / sign in**

   Go to [apex1.us/signin](https://apex1.us/signin) and enter your email to get a code — no password needed. Your account is created on first sign-in.

2. **Top up**

   Top up under **Billing** in the console: $0.50 minimum, by card, Alipay or WeChat Pay. Your balance is charged by the minute; we never charge your card automatically.

3. **Create a machine**

   Click **New machine** and choose:

   - **Machine spec**: S (2 vCPU / 4 GB) or M (4 vCPU / 8 GB).
   - **Pre-installed tools**: *AI coding tools* (Claude Code, Codex, Node, Docker and more) or *Bare system*.
   - **Claude account**: *Use my own* at no charge, or **Claude Pro** (+$40 per 30 days) that we sign in for you.

   Paste your **SSH public key** (no key yet? run `ssh-keygen -t ed25519` locally and `cat ~/.ssh/id_ed25519.pub`). The machine is ready in about 15 seconds; the pre-installed tools take about two more minutes.

4. **Open the SSH port and connect**

   On the machine page, under **External access**, open port **22** inside the machine. You get an external port between 20000 and 29999. Then:

   ```bash
   ssh -p <external-port> dev@<server-ip>
   ```

   The machine page has the exact command ready to copy. See [Connect over SSH](/getting-started/connect/).

5. **Start Claude Code**

   ```bash
   cd ~/workspace
   claude
   ```

   On first run it asks you to sign in: with your own account follow [Sign in to Claude](/claude/sign-in/); if you bought Pro, just use it once we've signed it in.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
	New machines **stay up by default**: billed by running time, never more than the monthly cap. To save while idle, turn on [auto-suspend](/machines/auto-standby/) at creation or on the machine page.
</Aside>
