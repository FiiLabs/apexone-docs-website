If you are paying for a Claude or ChatGPT subscription and not using all of it, you can share the
idle capacity through ApexOne. For a limited time we will also pay you to get started.

This page is the full rule set. It is the same program announced by email — written out here so you
can check the details before connecting anything.

<Aside type="tip" title="Open now">
	The program runs until the rewards are claimed. Every tier has a limited number of slots,
	allocated in the order accounts are connected. **Your own clock starts the day your account goes
	online** — not from a fixed date — so connecting today means your 10-day reward lands ten days
	from today.
</Aside>

## Rewards

| Keep your account online for | You receive |
| ---------------------------- | ----------- |
| **10 days**                  | **5 USDT**  |
| 30 days                      | +20 USDT    |
| 60 days                      | +25 USDT    |
| 90 days                      | +50 USDT    |
| **Total**                    | **up to 100 USDT per account** |

Rewards are cumulative: reach 90 days and you will have received all four. They are paid **on top of**
your normal revenue share, which keeps accruing the whole time.

<Aside type="caution" title="Per account, not per person">
	The 100 USDT ceiling applies to **one connected account**. You may connect up to two accounts,
	so one person can reach 200 USDT — but each account earns its own rewards on its own clock.
</Aside>

## Who can join

<Steps>

1. **New contributors only.** The program is open to anyone who had no supply account with us before
   it started. If you have already connected an account, your existing earnings continue as usual,
   but this particular program does not apply to you.

2. **A paid subscription is required.** Free accounts cannot be onboarded — the onboarding probe
   checks that the account can actually serve production traffic, and free tiers fail it.

3. **Claude and ChatGPT accounts are both eligible**, on the same reward schedule.

4. **Up to two accounts per person.**

</Steps>

## How the day counter works

This is the part worth reading carefully, because it is the only thing the rewards depend on.

<Steps>

1. **Connect your account.** One OAuth step — no API key, no password. See
   [Share Your Subscription](/earn/share-subscription/) for the click-by-click walkthrough.

2. **Pass the onboarding probe.** We send a short probe to confirm the account can serve real
   traffic. This usually completes within the hour. The counter does not start until the account
   is in the pool.

3. **Stay online.** Your counter advances by one for every day the account is connected and
   available to take requests.

4. **If it goes offline, the counter pauses — it does not reset.** Bring the account back and the
   count continues from where it stopped. Disconnecting an account and connecting it again, however,
   starts a new account with a new counter.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="You do not need traffic to qualify">
	The counter tracks availability, not usage. An account that is connected and schedulable advances
	its counter even on a day when no request happens to be routed to it. What you cannot do is
	connect an account that cannot serve — an expired credential or a subscription that no longer has
	quota takes the account out of the pool, and the counter stops with it.
</Aside>

## Getting paid

Rewards land in the same contributor wallet as your revenue share, and follow the same rules:

- Balances become withdrawable after a **72-hour holding period**.
- Withdrawals pay out as **USDT on BNB Chain**, typically within minutes.
- **Zero withdrawal fees — we cover the gas.**

You will need a payout address bound to your account before you can withdraw. See
[Earnings & Withdrawals](/earn/earnings-and-withdrawals/) for that step.

## Slots and how they run out

Every tier has a fixed number of slots. They are allocated in the order accounts are connected, so
an account that has been online longer sits ahead of one connected later. When a tier's slots are
gone, that tier stops paying — the remaining tiers are unaffected.

There is no fixed end date. The program ends when the rewards are claimed, or if we need to stop it
for the reasons in the fine print below.

## The fine print

- You always control your account. Disconnect at any time from your dashboard; a short drain window
  lets in-flight requests finish cleanly.
- We commit to the revenue-share rate, the reward amounts and the thresholds on this page. We do
  **not** commit to any specific revenue-share earnings figure — that depends on how much traffic
  your account actually serves.
- You are responsible for deciding whether sharing your subscription quota is compatible with your
  own agreement with the upstream provider.
- We may pause or end this program if upstream provider policies change.
